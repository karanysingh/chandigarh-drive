Chandigarh Drive

Sukhna Lake and Sector 8 · half-scale preview

Start at the Sukhna Lake parking, head down Sukhna Path to Uttar Marg, and take Himalaya Marg or the sector road to Sector 8's inner market. Driving is on the left and roundabouts go clockwise. There's no timer; find the four postcard spots if you like. Press F to get out and walk: the Sukhna promenade and boat jetty are on foot.
